FHIR R4 Healthcare Data Domain: Core Resources

This document is a compact domain reference for HL7 FHIR R4 healthcare
data exchange.

Patient The Patient resource represents demographic and administrative
information about an individual receiving healthcare or other
health-related services. It is focused on the “who” of a healthcare
interaction. Common elements include identifiers, active status, name,
telecom information, gender, birth date, address, communication
preferences, and references to organizations or practitioners involved
in care.

Observation Observation represents measurements and simple assertions
about a patient, device, or other subject. Typical examples include
vital signs, laboratory results, body weight, height, and smoking
status. Observation is generally intended for point-in-time measurements
or findings rather than a diagnosis.

Condition Condition represents a clinical condition, problem, diagnosis,
or health concern that has reached a level of importance for clinical
tracking or management. It can represent a diagnosed disease, a
problem-list item, or a clinical concern.

MedicationRequest MedicationRequest represents an order or request for
medication and instructions for administration. It is used for
prescribing/requesting medication and is distinct from
MedicationStatement, which records medication use or a reported history
of taking a medication.

Encounter Encounter represents an interaction between a patient and
healthcare provider(s) for providing healthcare services or assessing
health status. Encounters can occur in ambulatory, emergency, inpatient,
home-health, or virtual settings.

DiagnosticReport DiagnosticReport represents findings and interpretation
from diagnostic investigations. It can include clinical context, coded
or textual conclusions, references to individual Observations, and other
supporting information.

Important modeling distinction: - Patient describes who receives care. -
Encounter describes the healthcare interaction. - Observation describes
a measurement or simple finding. - Condition describes a diagnosis,
problem, or clinical concern. - MedicationRequest describes a medication
order/request. - DiagnosticReport describes a diagnostic investigation
and its interpretation.

Source: HL7 FHIR R4 specification, https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/
