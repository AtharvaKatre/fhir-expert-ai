FHIR R4 Domain Reference: Patient and Encounter Workflow

Patient

The Patient resource contains demographic and administrative information
about an individual receiving healthcare services. It is the central
“who” resource for many clinical records.

A patient may have records in multiple healthcare organizations. Patient
information can therefore be linked to encounters, observations,
conditions, medications, diagnostic reports, procedures, claims, and
many other resources.

Encounter

Encounter represents an interaction between a patient and one or more
healthcare providers for the purpose of providing healthcare services or
assessing health status.

Encounter can cover different settings: - ambulatory/outpatient -
emergency - inpatient - home health - virtual care

An encounter represents a lifecycle rather than only a single timestamp.
Depending on the setting, it can include pre-admission, admission, the
interaction itself, a stay, and discharge.

Relationships

A simplified clinical data relationship can look like:

Patient | +– Encounter | | | +– Observation | +– DiagnosticReport | +–
Condition | +– MedicationRequest | +– AllergyIntolerance +–
MedicationStatement

The Encounter provides context for many events. For example, an
Observation can identify the encounter during which a measurement was
taken, while a DiagnosticReport can identify the encounter associated
with the diagnostic investigation.

Important reasoning rule: Do not treat every piece of clinical
information as an Observation. FHIR separates resources according to
their meaning and workflow role. A measurement belongs naturally in
Observation, a diagnosis/problem in Condition, an interaction in
Encounter, and an individual’s allergy risk in AllergyIntolerance.
